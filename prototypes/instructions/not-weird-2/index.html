Kazimir Malevich

Kazimir Severinovich Malevich[nb 1] (23 February [O.S. 11 February] 1879[1] – 15 May 1935) was a Russian avant-garde artist and art theorist,[nb 2] whose work and writings pioneered the development of abstract painting in the 20th century.[2] He is best known as the founder of Suprematism, a radically non-objective form of painting he introduced in 1915.
